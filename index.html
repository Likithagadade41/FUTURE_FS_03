OUR STORY

It started with
one perfect scoop.

Melt & Swirl was born from a simple belief — the best moments don't need a reason. Sometimes, all you need is your favourite ice cream and someone to share it with.

We started with a small collection of handcrafted flavours, made with creamy ingredients, real fruits, rich chocolate and a whole lot of curiosity.

Today, every scoop we serve is still made with the same idea: keep it fresh, keep it joyful, and give people a reason to slow down and enjoy the moment.
